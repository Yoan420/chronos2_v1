Errors and snapshot badges show forecast − observed, in €/MWh. Green ↑ means above the observed price; red ↓ means below it. These colors indicate direction, not forecast quality.

Daily means require every physical hour of the delivery day. Missing or incomplete series display —. Models Mean ± std combines the two complete daily forecast means (Model and Storm), using their population standard deviation.

Definitions, coverage and simulation assumptions

Each window ends on the last complete observed local day available at or before the report date, independently per country. Model and Storm are scored on exactly the same finite observations. Missing prices are never replaced by zero. 60-MIN preserves all physical hours, including 23/25-hour DST days. DAY averages each complete physical day first, then scores those daily means with equal weight per day; it is not the average hourly MAE.

MAE = mean absolute error; BIAS = mean(forecast − observed); RMSE = root mean squared error, all in EUR/MWh. Hit Rate (±€5) counts |forecast − observed| ≤ 5 EUR/MWh, not directional accuracy or win rate against Storm. R² = 1 − Σ(error²)/Σ(observed − mean observed)²; it is unavailable for a constant observed sample.

Daily P&L is a hypothetical storage diagnostic, not a realized trading gain or the previous-day-reference EVA. For each complete matched day, each forecast separately chooses a charge/discharge schedule (maximum 1 MW, capacity 4 MWh). Starting and ending state of charge are zero. Charge and discharge efficiencies both equal √0.85; simultaneous charging and discharging is forbidden. The fixed schedule is then valued at observed hourly prices, never optimized using those observations. No fees, degradation, forecast-revision or execution costs are included. Only days with both valid schedules enter the mean. DAY retains this same hourly arbitrage simulation. The exact external dashboard implementation is unknown: numerical identity is not claimed.

Only locally available verified Model and Storm results are included. No other provider or 15-minute forecast is fabricated. Backtest-based history is retrospective, not proof of prospective execution. No model is trained or changed by this report.
