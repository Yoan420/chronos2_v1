2.4. Conclusions
Overall, the existing literature has separately examined point and probabilistic zero-shot fore-
casting, the use of exogenous information, and hybrid FM-based forecasting systems. However,
the evidence is based predominantly on short test periods, and no study reviewed above jointly
evaluates FMs against strong EPF-specific benchmarks in terms of point forecasting accuracy,
probabilistic forecasting accuracy, and economic value over a long test period. The empirical
analysis below is designed to address this gap.
