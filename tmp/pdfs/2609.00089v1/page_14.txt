for 15 consecutive epochs. Note that Marcjasz et al. [22] used a slightly different setup: 1000
hyperparameter-tuning trials, variable selection during hyperparameter optimization, the Adam
optimizer, no learning-rate scheduler, an upper limit of 1024 neurons (rather than 512), and batch
normalization. Our setup is computationally faster while retaining comparable predictive accu-
racy. Furthermore, instead of the moment-based parameterization, we use the original formulation
of the Johnson’s SU distribution, with location parameterξ, scale parameterλ, and shape param-
etersγandδ[53]. Although these parameters cannot be directly interpreted as moments of the
distribution, they allow quantile forecasts to be obtained analytically from the estimated parame-
ters.
