To formally compare the predictive performance of a foundation model with that of a benchmark,
we apply the test ofconditional predictive ability[CPA; 58]. Following Lago et al. [27] and
Lipiecki et al. [48], we use its multivariate variant tailored to the 24-dimensional structure of
day-ahead electricity price forecasts. The test aggregates the hourly loss differentials within each
day and is applied separately using the daily MAE, MSE, and CRPS. In each case, the one-sided
alternative is that the foundation model yields a lower expected loss than the benchmark, either
LEAR+CP or DDNN-JSU.
