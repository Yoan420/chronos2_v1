In the QB strategy, the trader seeks to generate a profit on daydby buying electricity at hour
hb, charging the battery, and subsequently discharging it and selling the electricity at hourhs >h b;
see Figure 3. In contrast to the strategy of Uniejewski [57], we assume that the trader submits a
so-calledloop bid, also referred to as aloop blockat EPEX SPOT; seehttps://www.epexspot.
com/en/tradingproducts. The loop bid consists of two coupled orders that are either both
executed or both rejected. Consequently, there is no need to settle a partially executed pair of
orders in the intraday or balancing market [12], to trade using only a part of the battery capacity
[13, 57, 59], or to replace the limit orders with unlimited bids [22, 60].
