6.3.2. Profits from BESS arbitrage
The economic rankings are less stable and depend strongly on the value ofα, the decision
parameter of the quantile-based strategies. For Germany and Poland, TabPFN-TS-3 generates the
highest total profit for the three least risk-averse levels, i.e.,α=90-70%. For the levels of 60%
and 50%, DDNN-JSU dominates the economic evaluation in these markets. For Spain, DDNN-
JSU performs best for all but the highest level,α=90%. Overall, the QB results reveal a clear
transition with the risk level. TabPFN variants tend to perform best under the more risky strategies,
whereas DDNN-JSU becomes increasingly competitive as the strategy becomes more risk-averse.

are small. For example, in Poland, the unlimited-bid profits of TabPFN-3 and TabPFN-TS-3 are
